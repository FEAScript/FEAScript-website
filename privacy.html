Privacy

What we measure, and what we don't.

This page covers feascript.com, docs.feascript.com, blog.feascript.com, studio.feascript.com and lab.feascript.com. There are no ads and no cookies, and we never sell or share data with anyone else.

Cookies

These sites set no cookies, neither ours nor anyone else's: the analytics below works without them, and the pages embed no videos, ads or social media widgets. Your settings and progress are kept in your browser's local storage instead (see below). That is why there is no cookie banner.

When you sign up for the newsletter, the form opens a Mailchimp page in a new tab. That page is Mailchimp's, under its own privacy policy, and may set its own cookies.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages people read. It records the page you visit, the site that sent you, your browser, operating system and type of device, your country, and how fast the page loaded. It never receives your name or email address.

It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, so it cannot tell new visitors from returning ones or follow you from site to site. Cloudflare processes this data under its own privacy policy.

To opt out, use a content blocker. Every page works the same without analytics.

What stays in your browser

Your theme choice, your progress and scores in Tri's Lab, your FEM Blocks workspaces and your work in Studio are kept in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to us. Clearing the site data in your browser removes them.

Newsletter and email

If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is stored by Mailchimp, which sends it. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. If you email info@feascript.com, we use your message only to reply to you.

Fonts and scripts from other services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and libraries, including FEAScript itself, from jsDelivr and cdnjs. Your browser fetches these files directly, so those services see your IP address, as any website you load a file from does.

Questions

Ask us anything about this page at info@feascript.com. If what we collect changes, we update this page first.
